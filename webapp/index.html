Fadfid (فَضْفَضَ)

A place to open your heart and talk freely

A wellbeing support companion that respects your culture and keeps your words confidential.

  1. Fadfid is a support companion, not therapy, and never diagnoses.
  2. Your conversation is not saved. It disappears when you leave.
  3. If you are in danger, Fadfid will stay with you, share trusted help (999, 16000), and offer a trained counsellor.
  4. Confidential, with one limit: Fadfid can't help hide a plan to hurt someone. If you talk about hurting someone, it will ask you to talk with a counsellor, who would ask who you are only if you agree. Fadfid itself doesn't know who you are.

Fadfid is for adults (18+). You'll do a quick age check next.

Research prototype built by CMU-Q students (67-341 Generative AI & Governance). Not a medical or emergency service. In an emergency call 999.
